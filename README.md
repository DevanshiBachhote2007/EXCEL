# 📊 Data Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview
This project analyzes **200+ customer transactions** across multiple product categories, regions, and payment methods.  
Using advanced Excel features such as **Pivot Tables, Scenario Manager, Goal Seek, Regression Analysis, and Dashboards**, the workbook uncovers insights into **revenue trends, customer behavior, and product performance**.

The goal is to demonstrate how Excel can be used for **data intelligence and storytelling**, turning raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

---

## ✨ Features
- **Data Cleaning & Filtering**: Multiple value filtering, timestamp calculations.
- **Scenario Analysis**: Scenario Manager and Goal Seek for pricing and quantity decisions.
- **Statistical Modeling**: Linear Regression for predictive insights.
- **Pivot Tables & Charts**: Summarized views by product, region, customer, payment method, and month.
- **Interactive Dashboard**: A storytelling layout with revenue, regional, customer, product, payment, and time insights.

---

## 📂 Dataset
Each transaction includes:
- Transaction ID, Date, Customer details
- Product ID, Category, Segment
- Quantity, Unit Price, Total Price
- Payment Method, Region

**Example record:**
TRX0155 | 11-04-2024 | Paul Baker | Monitor | Electronics | Qty: 3 | ₹249.99 | Cash | Central | Basic | Total: ₹749.97

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Revenue Engine**: Electronics dominate with ₹171,756 (75% of total revenue).  
- **Regional Performance**: East leads with ₹59,288, while South lags at ₹36,398.  
- **Customer Loyalty**: Top customers (Mark Carter, Edward Mitchell, Barbara Young) contribute significantly.  
- **Payment Preferences**: Credit Card and PayPal are equally popular; Cash remains strong.  
- **Seasonality**: Sales peak in April and January; lowest in September and November.
